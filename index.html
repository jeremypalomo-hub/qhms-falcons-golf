The team

South Charlotte, first tee.

The Falcons play for Quail Hollow Middle School. Golf is a fall sport here — nine holes, midweek afternoons. Matches are at Sunset Hills Golf Course, by the airport.

Practice lives at First Tee Greater Charlotte, where the team is learning the game the slow way: posture, patience, and a bag they carry themselves.

Match rules

How we score it.

  • 4 golfers each week · 2 foursomes. Best 3 scores count for the team total.
  • Double par is max — pick up if not in the hole.
  • Everyone plays from the red tees. Carry your own bag. Own clubs. No carts, caddies, rangefinders, or parental help.
  • Parents stay on or near the cart path. All stakes played as red — drop and 1 stroke.

QHMS student-athlete contract

How we play, off the course too.

Falcons are held to a stricter code than non-athletes, on campus and off. Playing means you agree to this contract and CMS behavior policy. Golf notes below follow the school sheet.

Absences

Show up, or tell Coach.

  • Excused (doctor, illness, death in the family, injury, school activity): no penalty for a missed practice or match.
  • Unexcused means the coach was not told. Next-game playing time is Coach Daneri’s call.
  • Miss more than half the school day and you sit that day’s practice or match.

Minor

Detentions, ISS, or hurting the team.

Two lunch detentions, one after-school detention, ISS, or conduct detrimental to the team.

  • 1st — talk with coach/AD, parent contact, verbal warning.
  • 2nd — sit the first half of a match.
  • 3rd — one full match (golf).
  • 4th — off the team.

Major

Bullying, fighting, OSS.

Immediate suspension. A conference with the AD, administration, and coach has to happen before anyone comes back.

  • 1st — two matches (golf).
  • 2nd, or two OSS/ISS incidents in a season — permanent removal.

Zero tolerance

Harassment, assault, drugs, alcohol, weapons.

Immediate removal. No reinstatement. A suspension on one QHMS team and another later in the year also ends sports for the rest of the year.
